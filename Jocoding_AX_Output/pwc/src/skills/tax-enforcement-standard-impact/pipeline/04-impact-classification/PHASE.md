# Phase 4. Impact Classification

## Purpose

Classify why a matched enforcement standard may need review.

## Step 1. Assign Main Impact Type

Use exactly one main type:

- Direct article impact.
- Condition impact.
- Target impact.
- Effect impact.
- Exception impact.
- Timing impact.
- Low relevance.
- Hold for review.

Add secondary types only when needed.

## Step 2. Assign Confidence

High:

- Direct article match.
- Legal logic match.
- Evidence exists.

Medium:

- No direct article match, but logic match and evidence exist.

Low:

- Keyword or semantic match with weak evidence.

Hold:

- Missing article, timing, or rule paragraph evidence.

## Step 3. Link Evidence

Use short evidence summaries:

- Changed article.
- Enforcement standard number.
- Related article.
- Main rule paragraph summary.
- Supporting interpretation revision case.

Do not quote long source text.

## Step 4. Remove or Hold Low-Relevance Items

Exclude:

- Wrong tax type.
- Wrong taxpayer type.
- Wrong transaction type.
- No related article and no rule evidence.
- Keyword-only match.

Hold:

- Related issue but missing date.
- Related article but unclear effect.
- Enforcement standard has examples but no rule paragraph.

## Step 5. Generate Review Question

Question should ask what a tax professional must verify.

Examples:

- Does the changed condition alter the rule paragraph of this enforcement standard?
- Does the effective date affect transactions covered by this standard?
- Does the exception newly include or exclude the taxpayer type in this standard?

Do not produce final tax advice.
